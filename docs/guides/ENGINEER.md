Q-TECH CRM: ENGINEER (one page)

You do not see prices, payments or costs. That is normal.
Sign in from the home-screen icon. Bottom bar: Today, RFQs, Orders, Companies.

1. START WITH TODAY
 - Tap Today. It lists your jobs: the orders you are an engineer on, and the goods on your own orders. One list, no tabs.
 - "Commissioning on <day>" (or "today", "tomorrow"): a site visit in the next 5 working days.
 - "Goods delivered to site: set the commissioning date": the goods are there, pick the day.
 - "Overdue or due today" is what needs you now. Tap a row, then "Open ORD-..." to see the order.

2. CAPTURE A PHOTO OR A MESSAGE (30 seconds)
 - Tap Capture (the + button), or share a photo or WhatsApp text to the CRM from your phone.
 - Choose "Choose photos", or "Tap and hold here, then tap Paste" for copied text.
 - Pick where it goes: "On which RFQ" or "On which order". Search by customer name if it is not listed under "Recent".
 - Tap Save. That is all. No other fields.
 - No signal? It says "You are offline". Try again when you have signal.
 - Wrong place? Tap Undo.

3. SITE QUESTIONS
 - If the customer asked something on site, capture it the same way and choose the RFQ.
 - Open questions show on the RFQ under "Open questions".

4. GOODS DELIVERED
 - Tap Orders, open the order.
 - Under "Procurement legs" find the leg.
 - Tap "Delivered today". One tap. The date is today's.
 - Delivered on another day? Tap the date and change it.
 - Goods late? Today shows it. Tap "Chase goods" to get the message.

5. COMMISSIONING DONE
 - Open the order. In "Commissioning" check "Planned on".
 - When finished tap "Mark done today". One tap.
 - Different date? "Change planned date".
 - Site notes: "Edit notes", then "Save notes".

6. IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
 - Do not fix prices or customers yourself. Capture a note on the order and tell the owner.

Keep it short. Photo and one tap is enough.
