Q-TECH CRM: ENGINEER (one page)

You do not see payments, costs or margins. That is normal.
Sign in from the home-screen icon. Bottom bar: Today, RFQs, Orders, Companies.

1. START WITH TODAY
 - Today lists your jobs: the orders you are an engineer on, and the goods on your own orders. One list, no tabs.
 - "Commissioning on <day>" (or "today", "tomorrow"): a site visit in the next 5 working days.
 - "Goods delivered to site: set the commissioning date": the goods are there, pick the day.
 - Red means today or late. Tap a row, then "Open ORD-..." to see the order.

2. PASTE OR PHOTO (30 seconds)
 - Tap "Paste or photo", or share a photo or WhatsApp text to the CRM from your phone.
 - Pick where it goes: the RFQ or the order. Search by customer name if it is not listed.
 - Tap Save. No other fields. Wrong place? Tap Undo.

3. SITE QUESTIONS
 - The customer asked something on site? Save it the same way, on the RFQ.
 - Open questions show on the RFQ under "Open questions".

4. GOODS DELIVERED
 - Open the order. Under "Supplier orders" tap "Delivered today". One tap.
 - Another day? Tap the date and change it.

5. COMMISSIONING
 - Open the order. When finished tap "Mark done today". One tap.
 - The day moved? "Change planned date".
 - Site notes and the site contact are on the order.

6. IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
 - Do not fix prices or customers yourself. Save a note on the order and tell the owner.

Keep it short. A photo and one tap is enough.
