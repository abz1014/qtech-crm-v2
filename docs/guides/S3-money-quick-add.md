# Q-Tech CRM: quick add, Other, I paid and cash in hand (S-3)

Admin for quick add, Other and cash in hand; engineers for **[I paid]**. Words in **[brackets]** are on the screen.

## Add an expense in three taps

1. Press **E** (computer, outside a text box), or tap **[+ New]** then **[Expense]**, or go to **Money**, **Expenses**. **[Quick add]** opens.
2. Type the amount. Tap what it was for (the six buttons are the ones you use most; **[More…]** lists the rest).
3. Tap **[Save]**. The row stays open for the next one, and the category stays chosen: a second spend of the same kind is just the amount and Enter.

Cash or bank comes from the category. A salary, rent or internet bill paid by the 10th says **[This bill is for]** last month: change it if needed. Optional: **[Paid on]**, a **[Detail]**, a **[Receipt]** photo, or **[Has a GST invoice]** for the GST.

Many receipts at once? **[Add expenses]** gives one row each, then **[Save N expenses]**.

A supplier is paid with **[Pay]** on the supplier order, not here, so **[We owe suppliers]** always goes down.

## Other, with a title

Not in any category? Do not invent one.

1. In **[Quick add]**, type the amount and tap **[Other]**.
2. Write **[What was it for?]**, for example "Courier from shop to office". It cannot be left empty.
3. Tap **[Save]**. The expense report lists each Other by what you wrote.
4. When one title comes back three times, a banner asks if it should be a category: **[Make it a category]**.

## I paid (engineers)

You paid for something yourself.

1. Tap **[I paid]** (next to the big **[Photo or note]** button on your phone, or under your initials).
2. Type **[How much, PKR]**, and if you like **[What for]** and the **[Job]**.
3. Tap **[Photo of the receipt]** and take the photo.
4. Tap **[Send for approval]**.

**[My claims]** shows Waiting, Approved or Not approved. Sent the wrong one? **[Take back]** while it is waiting.

### Approving claims (admin)

An **[I paid: N to approve]** row appears on To do. Open **Money**, **Expenses**, **[To approve]**: open the receipt, pick the **[Category]** and how it was **[Paid back]**, then **[Approve]**. It becomes an expense from then on. Not right? **[Not approved…]** and say why; the engineer sees it. A claim waiting is in no total.

## Cash in hand

1. **Money**, **Expenses**: the **[Cash in hand]** line shows what should be in the office cash.
2. The first time: **[Set opening cash]** and type what is in the box.
3. Took cash out of the bank for the office? **[Cash drawn]** and the amount. It is not an expense.
4. To check: count the box, tap **[Count cash]**, type the amount, **[Save count]**.
5. Short or over? The difference is kept with the count. Look for a cash expense not recorded yet.

Every cash expense lowers what should be there.

## Monthly chores on To do (admin)

- **Month closing**, from the 1st to the 10th: bank check, monthly bills, **[Lock a month]**.
- **GST to FBR**, from the 1st until paid: grey to the 12th, amber from the 13th, red after the 15th. Pay by the 15th, return by the 18th.
