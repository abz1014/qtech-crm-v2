# Q-Tech CRM: the engineer's phone (S-5)

For engineers. Words in **[brackets]** are on the screen. **You never see prices, costs, payments or margins.** That is normal.

## Put it on your phone

1. Open the Q-Tech CRM address in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) and sign in with your email and password. Forgot the password? Ask the admin.
2. Android: tap your initials, then **[Install app]**. iPhone: tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the Q-Tech icon from now on. Text too small? Your initials, then **[Text size]**, **[Larger]**.

## The bottom bar

Two places and a big button:

- **[To do]**: your jobs by day. Red means today or late.
- **[Jobs]**: your jobs first, then every job.
- **[Photo or note]** (the big button in the middle): a photo or a short note on a job.
- **[I paid]** (beside it): money you paid yourself.

## A day on site

1. **Open [To do].** Each row is a job or goods for you. Tap the row for the site, the contact and the notes.
2. **Goods arrived?** Open the job and tap **[Delivered today]**. You ordered from the supplier yourself? **[I placed this order]**.
3. **Plan the visit:** tap **[Change planned date]**, pick the **[Planned date]**, save.
4. **Job finished?** Tap **[Mark done today]**. One tap. Wrong tap? **[Undo]** at the bottom.
5. **Site photo or note:** tap **[Photo or note]**, take the photo or type, pick the job, save.
6. **Paid for something?** Tap **[I paid]**: **[How much, PKR]**, **[What for]**, the **[Job]**, **[Photo of the receipt]**, then **[Send for approval]**. The office approves it and pays you back.

## Something looks wrong on a job

Do not change it. Tap **[Photo or note]**, save a short note or a photo on the job, and tell the owner.

## No signal

You can read what is already on the phone. Saving needs signal: the app says so plainly. Try again when you have it.

## What you can and cannot see

| You can | You cannot |
|---|---|
| Your jobs, sites, contacts, goods, installation dates, notes and photos | Prices, quotation totals, payments, costs, margins |
| Mark goods delivered and a job done | Create invoices or record payments |
| Send an **[I paid]** claim | Approve a claim |
