# Q-Tech CRM: the new menu and the new words (S-1)

For everyone: admin, sales and engineers. Words in **[brackets]** are the exact button or label on the screen. This replaces the old "start using it" sheet.

## The menu

The top bar (a computer) and the bottom bar (a phone) now have **four places**:

| Place | What it is | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **[To do]** | The one list that says "do this". Late, Today, This week. | Everyone |
| **[Jobs]** | Every customer request from enquiry to payment, one page each. | Everyone |
| **[Money]** | What to collect, invoices, expenses, locking a month, reports. | Admin and sales |
| **[Companies]** | Customers and suppliers. | Admin and sales |

An engineer sees only **[To do]** and **[Jobs]**, and a big **[Photo or note]** button (see the engineer phone guide, S-5).

Beside them: **[+ New]** (start something) and **[Search or jump to]** (press Ctrl+K on a computer).

### Where did RFQs and Orders go?

They are **[Jobs]** now. One customer request is one job: the enquiry, the quotation, the order, the supplier orders, the delivery, the invoice and the payment are all on its page. A job is called "Job 139" (the old RFQ number) or "Job O-45" (an order that never had an enquiry).

- Old links and bookmarks still work: an RFQ or order address opens its job.
- The old RFQ and Order pages are gone (removed 30 October). An old link or bookmark to one still works: it opens the job.
- Searching "RFQ", "order" or an old number still finds the job.

## The numbers

- **Only [To do] shows a number.** It is how many things need you today (Late plus Today). The same number is at the top of the To do page.
- **[Money] shows a red dot, no number,** only while an invoice is past its due date.
- Red means late money or a promise to a customer that slipped. Amber means due today or soon. Grey means old or not urgent.

## Words that changed

| You used to see | Now |
|---|---|
| Today | **[To do]** |
| RFQs, Orders | **[Jobs]** |
| New RFQ | **[New enquiry]** |
| Who owes us | **[To collect]** |
| Invoices & GST | **[Invoices]** |
| Month end, Close through | **[Lock a month]** (one month at a time) |
| Match bank statement | **[Bank check]** |
| Things to check | **[Mistakes to fix]** |
| Sent to customer (TCS) | **[Invoice posted]** |
| Tax deposited | **[GST paid to FBR]** |

The old word still works in Search.

## Your To do list in one minute

1. Tap **[To do]**. Red **[Late]** rows first, then **[Today]**, then **[This week]**.
2. Every row has its own button: **[Chase]**, **[Copy reminder]**, **[Send quotation]**, **[Ask suppliers]**, **[Create invoice]** and so on. Tap it to do that step.
3. Not now? Tap the snooze icon. **[Not ours to chase]** hides it until the job changes.
4. At most seven rows show; **[N more this week]** holds the rest. Nothing is dropped.
5. Grey lines at the bottom: **[Chased, waiting]**, **[Older than 30 days]**, **[Imported, to review]**. They are never counted.
6. Admin only: **[Mine]** and **[Team]** switch between your list and everyone else's (read only), and **[Weekly review]** is the Monday decision page.

## Shortcuts (computer, outside a text box)

**N** new enquiry, **E** add an expense (admin), **O** an order without an enquiry, **G then T** to-do, **/** jump to a list's search, **?** the full list.

## Text size

Tap your initials, then **[Text size]**: **[Smaller]**, **[Normal]** or **[Larger]**. It changes this device only.
