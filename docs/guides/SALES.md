Q-TECH CRM: SALES, DAILY (one page)

Sign in on your phone from the home-screen icon. Bottom bar: Today, RFQs, Orders, Companies, Money, Reports.

1. START WITH TODAY
 - Tap Today. Read "Overdue or due today" first, then "Ready to move".
 - Tabs: All, Bids, Sourcing, Money.
 - Tap a row. The message is already written. Tap "Copy and mark chased" (or "Copy and mark reminded"), paste it into WhatsApp, send.
 - Not now? Tap the clock icon (Snooze), pick a date.

2. NEW ENQUIRY: PASTE IT
 - Tap RFQs, then New RFQ.
 - Customer: pick the company (and Site if asked).
 - Paste what the customer sent into "Write the enquiry". Nothing is saved yet.
 - Check the items. Spec lines blank is fine.
 - Tap "Looks right: find suppliers".

3. FLOAT TO SUPPLIERS
 - Tick the suppliers you want (the app pre-ticks the usual ones).
 - Tap "Copy & mark floated to N".
 - Open each supplier chat in WhatsApp, paste, send.
 - Wrong supplier? "Float undone" appears: tap Undo.
 - Later, to ask more suppliers: open the RFQ, tap "Chase suppliers" or "Float to another supplier".

4. SUPPLIER REPLIES
 - Open the RFQ. Under supplier replies tap "Paste what they sent" and paste their message.
 - Tap "Read it". Check the price, currency, lead time.
 - Accept it. Nothing is saved until you accept.
 - No offer? Mark "No price".
 - Photo or PDF from the supplier: use Capture (the + button), "Which RFQ?", done.

5. QUOTE THE CUSTOMER
 - On the RFQ tap "Issue quotation".
 - Enter "Unit price, net PKR" for each item. GST is 18% or Exempt per line.
 - Check Validity, Delivery, Payment terms.
 - Tap Preview PDF, then Download PDF. Send the PDF on WhatsApp.
 - Tap "Mark bid sent" if you sent it another way.

6. CHASE
 - Customer quiet? Today shows it. Open it, tap "Copy and mark chased".
 - Or on the RFQ tap "Chase customer". After 3 chases with no answer, the app asks if you want to close it.
 - Close an RFQ: "Close RFQ because" and pick the reason.

7. WON: CONVERT TO ORDER
 - On the RFQ tap "Mark won".
 - Each item shows its best-ranked supplier. Change it only if needed.
 - Enter "Customer PO number" and "Payment terms in days".
 - Tap "Create order". The order opens.

8. INVOICE
 - Open the order. In Invoicing tap "Create invoice" (or Money, GST register, "New invoice").
 - Check Invoice number (it continues the series), Invoice date, Net amount, GST.
 - Save. Then send the invoice to the customer.
 - Courier sent? In the GST register tap "Sent by TCS today".

9. MONEY, ORDER BALANCES
 - Money, "Order balances": who owes what. Overdue ones show a "Payment reminder": tap "Copy and mark reminded".

Stuck? Search is at the top. Tell the owner what confused you; never log extra detail.
