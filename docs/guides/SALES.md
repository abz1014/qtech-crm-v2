Q-TECH CRM: SALES, DAILY (one page)

Sign in on your phone from the home-screen icon. Bottom bar: Today, RFQs, Orders, Companies, Money.
A number beside a tab means "this many need you".
Press ? on a computer to see the keyboard shortcuts.

1. START WITH TODAY
 - Tap Today. Read "Overdue or due today" first, then "Ready to move".
 - Tabs: All, Quotes, Suppliers, Money.
 - Tap a row. The message is already written. Tap "Copy message and mark chased" (or "... mark reminded"), paste it into WhatsApp, send.
 - WhatsApp or Email: pick once, the app remembers.
 - Not now? Tap Snooze, pick a date.

2. NEW ENQUIRY: PASTE IT
 - Tap RFQs, then New RFQ.
 - Customer: pick the company. Type their own reference (their RFQ number) under it: search finds the RFQ by it later.
 - Paste what the customer sent anywhere on the page. It splits into items. Nothing is saved yet, but it is kept on this phone:
   leave and come back, and "Continue your draft" brings it back.
 - Check the items. Spec lines blank is fine.
 - Tap "Looks right: find suppliers".

3. ASK SUPPLIERS
 - Tick the suppliers you want (the app pre-ticks the usual ones).
 - Tap "Write the message", then "Copy message for N suppliers".
 - Open each supplier chat in WhatsApp, paste, send. (Email instead? Tap "Email instead"; the app remembers.)
 - Wrong supplier? Tap Undo on the message at the bottom.
 - Later, to ask more suppliers: open the RFQ, tap "Ask more suppliers".

4. SUPPLIER REPLIES
 - Open the RFQ. Under "Replies from suppliers", each supplier still owing a price has its boxes open.
 - Type the price for each item, or tap "Paste reply" and check what it read. Nothing is saved until you save or accept.
 - Supplier says no? Tap the reason under "Can't quote".
 - Photo or PDF from the supplier: tap "Paste or photo", "Which RFQ?", done.
 - The best price for each item is marked "Recommended". Tap the arrow on a line to see why.

5. QUOTE THE CUSTOMER
 - On the RFQ tap the green "Issue quotation" (no supplier price yet? It is in More, and you can type your own price).
 - Enter "Unit price, net PKR" for each item. GST is 18% or Exempt per line.
 - Check Validity, Delivery, Payment terms.
 - Tap Preview PDF if you want, then "Issue quotation", then Share (or Download PDF). Send the PDF on WhatsApp.
 - Quoted another way (phone, email)? More, "Mark quotation sent".
 - Change it later: "Revise quotation". The first one stays as it was.

6. CHASE
 - Customer quiet? Today shows it. Open it, tap "Copy message and mark chased".
 - Or on the RFQ, under "Questions for the customer", tap "Copy chase text". After 3 chases with no answer, the app asks if you want to close it.
 - Close an RFQ: More, then pick the reason under "Close the RFQ because".

7. WON: CONVERT TO ORDER
 - On the RFQ tap the green "Mark won" (it turns green once the quotation is out).
 - Each item shows its best-ranked supplier. Change it only if needed.
 - Enter "Customer PO number" and "Payment terms in days".
 - Tap "Create order". The order opens.

8. INVOICE
 - Open the order. In Invoicing tap "Create invoice" (or Money, "Invoices & GST", "Create invoice").
 - Check Invoice number (it continues the series), Invoice date, Net amount, GST.
 - Save. Then send the invoice to the customer.
 - Courier sent? In "Invoices & GST" tick the invoices, then "Sent to customer today". The (i) explains TCS, FBR and PSID.

9. MONEY: WHO OWES US
 - Money opens on "Who owes us": what each order still owes. Overdue is in red.
 - Tap an overdue order: the "Payment reminder" is written. Tap "Copy and mark reminded".
 - Payments are recorded by the admin.
 - "Reports" (top right of Money): wins, asking suppliers, how fast customers answer, who comes back.

Stuck? Search is at the top. Tell the owner what confused you; never log extra detail.
