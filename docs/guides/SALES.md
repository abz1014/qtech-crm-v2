Q-TECH CRM: SALES, DAILY (one page)

Sign in from the home-screen icon. Bottom bar: Today, RFQs, Orders, Companies, Money.
A number on a tab always means "this many need you".

1. START WITH TODAY
 - Read "Overdue or due today" first. Tabs: All, Quotations, Suppliers & delivery, Money.
 - Tap a row. The message is already written. Tap "Copy message and mark chased" (or "... mark reminded"), paste it into WhatsApp, send.
 - WhatsApp or Email: pick once, the app remembers.
 - Not now? Tap Snooze and pick a day.

2. NEW ENQUIRY
 - RFQs, New RFQ. Or tap "Paste or photo" with the customer's message or photo.
 - Pick the company. Paste the items. Check them.
 - Tap the button to find suppliers.

3. ASK SUPPLIERS
 - The usual suppliers are already ticked. Tap "Copy message for N suppliers".
 - Paste it into each supplier's chat and send.
 - Later, to ask more: open the RFQ, "Ask suppliers".

4. SUPPLIER PRICES
 - Open the RFQ. "Paste what they sent", then "Read it", then accept. Nothing is saved until you accept.
 - The supplier has no price? "Can't quote".

5. QUOTATION
 - On the RFQ tap "Issue quotation". Price each item (tap a suggested price or type one).
 - "Preview PDF" if you want, then "Issue quotation", then Share or Download PDF.
 - Quoted by phone? Tap "Quotation sent". To change it later: "Revise quotation".

6. WON OR LOST
 - Won: "Mark won". Check the supplier per item and the customer PO. "Create order".
 - Lost or no reply: "Close" and pick the reason.
 - "Repeat a past order" (Orders) copies an old order. New orders come from a won RFQ.

7. INVOICE
 - Open the order, "Create invoice". Check the number, date and amounts. Save.

8. MONEY
 - Money opens on "Who owes us". Overdue is in red.
 - Tap an overdue order: the reminder is written. "Copy and mark reminded", paste, send.
 - Payments are recorded by the admin.
 - "Invoices & GST": your invoices, and whether the courier delivered them.
 - "Reports" (top right of Money): wins, asking suppliers, how fast customers answer, who comes back.

Stuck? Use Search at the top (it also finds Settings). Tell the owner what confused you; never log extra detail.
