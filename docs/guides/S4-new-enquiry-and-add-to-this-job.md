# Q-Tech CRM: New enquiry and Add to this job (S-4)

> **Placeholders.** These two actions are being built (redesign phase 4). Until they land, this page describes the plan; the words in [brackets] may change. Paste or photo still works as before. Update this guide when they ship.

## New enquiry

One way in: **[+ New]**, then **[New enquiry]** (or press **N**), or drop a file anywhere on To do or Jobs.

1. Drop or pick a file, take a photo, or paste the text of the customer's email or message.
2. Check what was read, side by side with the source: customer (with the reason it was chosen), contact, their reference, reply-by date, items and quantities. Unsure parts say **[Check this]**.
3. Tap the one green button, **[Create job N]**. The next step on the new job is **[Ask suppliers]**.

## Add to this job

On a job's page, a supplier's reply, a site photo, a customer's answer or a note goes straight to that job.

1. Open the job and tap **[Add]**. Paste, pick a file or take a photo.
2. The app guesses the kind (for example "Supplier price, from a named supplier") and you change it with one tap.
3. A supplier reply is read into prices. Check price, currency and lead time, then **[Accept N prices]**. The next step becomes **[Send quotation]**.

## What stays the same

- Pictures and PDFs are read on your device; nothing is sent anywhere to be read.
- Nothing is saved until you press the green button.
- After you copy a message to a supplier, the app asks **[Did you send it?]**. Only **[Yes, sent]** makes it say Sent; until then it says Asked.
