# Q-Tech CRM: New enquiry and Add to this job (S-4)

For sales and admin. Words in **[brackets]** are on the screen. These two actions replace the old Paste or photo sheet.

## New enquiry

One way in: tap **[New]**, then **[New enquiry]** (or press **N**). You can also drop the customer's PDF, photo or spreadsheet anywhere on **[To do]** or **[Jobs]**, or paste the message anywhere on the page.

1. The document is on the left and what was read is on the right. A PDF, a photo, a spreadsheet and pasted text are all read the same way, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere to be read.
2. Look at anything marked **[Check this]**: the customer (with the reason it was chosen), the contact, their reference, the reply-by date, the items and quantities.
3. Tap **[Use <company>]** when the customer is suggested, or choose the company.
4. Tap the one green button, **[Create job]**. The job opens. Nobody is asked for a price yet.
5. On the job, the next step is **[Ask suppliers]**. Tap it when you are ready.

Nothing is saved until you press **[Create job]**.

## Add to this job

On a job's page, a supplier's reply, a customer's answer, a site photo or a note goes straight to that job. The job is already known, so nothing asks where it goes.

1. Open the job. Copy the supplier's WhatsApp message, then paste it anywhere on the page. It lands in **[Add to this job]**. You can also drop or take a photo, or choose a file.
2. The app guesses what it is: **[Supplier price]**, **[Customer answer]**, **[Site photo]** or **[Note]**. Wrong guess? Tap the right one above the button.
3. For a supplier's price, check the supplier it names under **[From which supplier?]** and change it if it is wrong.
4. Look at the prices it read. Anything marked **[Check this]** is worth a second look. Untick one to leave it out.
5. Tap **[Accept N prices]**. The prices go on that supplier's row, and the next step becomes **[Send quotation]**.
6. A customer answering a question says **[Customer answer]**: tap **[Save answer]**.

Engineers can add only a photo or a note, never a price.

## Honest "Sent"

After you copy a message to a supplier, the app asks **[Did you send it?]**. Only **[Yes, sent]** makes it say Sent. Until then it says Asked.

## The next step on the job

The green button on a job says what is due. Today's words: **[Ask suppliers]**, **[Send quotation]**, **[Choose suppliers]**, **[Place the order]**, **[Deliver the goods]**, **[Create invoice]**, **[Chase the payment]**. Tap it and that stage opens on the same page.
