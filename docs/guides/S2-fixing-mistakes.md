# Q-Tech CRM: fixing a mistake (S-2)

You typed the wrong thing. Here is how to put it right, and what you cannot undo. Words in **[brackets]** are on the screen.

## The rule

- **You can fix what you entered yourself.** If it says "Entered by" someone else, ask them or the admin.
- **Most taps have [Undo]** for a few seconds at the bottom of the screen. Use it first.
- **A closed month is locked.** Reopen the month first (see below), then fix.
- **History is kept.** **[History]** on a record shows who changed what, and when.

## An enquiry or an item

1. Open the job. Tap **[More]**, then **[Edit RFQ details]**.
2. Change the customer, site, contact, customer reference or received date. Tap **[Save changes]**.
3. A wrong item: tap the three dots on it, then **[Edit item]** and **[Save item]**, or **[Remove item]**.
4. An item the order is built on stays until the order line is changed.

An admin can delete an enquiry entered by mistake. It shows what goes with it. **It is permanent.**

## A price, a quotation, a PO

Never change an issued document in place. Make a **revision**. The first one stays as history. The admin can delete the **latest** revision if it was never sent.

## An expense

1. **Money**, then **Expenses**. Open the expense's three dots, then change it or **[Delete]**. **[Undo]** is offered.
2. An expense in a locked month: reopen the month first.
3. A supplier payment is not an expense: it is changed on the supplier order (**[Pay]**).

## An invoice

1. **Money**, then **[Invoices]**, then the invoice. Change **[Invoice number]**, **[Invoice date]**, **[Net amount, PKR]** or **[GST, PKR]**.
2. **GST-filed warning.** If the month's GST return may already be filed, it says so. Tick **[I understand]** to save anyway, then file a revised return with FBR.
3. **[Delete invoice]** (admin) **cannot be undone.**

## A payment received

- Wrong amount, date or reference: on the order, under **[Invoices and payments]**, tap the three dots, then **[Correct]**. The old payment is kept, marked corrected.
- Recorded on the wrong order: **[Void]** it (give a reason), then record it on the right one. The record stays.
- An admin can **[Delete…]** a payment. **It is permanent.**
- A payment ticked in **[Bank check]** must be unticked first.

## Reopen a closed month first

1. **Money**, then **[Lock a month]**.
2. The newest closed month is reopened first; older ones follow, newest first.
3. Fix the entry, then lock the month again.

## What an admin delete means

Delete is for entries made by mistake. It removes the record and what hangs on it, and **there is no Undo after you confirm.** Void and Correct keep a record and are almost always better for money.

## If you are unsure

Do not guess. Send the owner a screenshot and one sentence: what you tried to do.
