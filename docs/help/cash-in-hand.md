1. Tap [Money], then [Expenses]. The [Cash in hand] line shows what should be in the office cash.
2. The first time, tap [Set opening cash] and type what is in the box.
3. Took cash out of the bank for the office? Tap [Cash drawn] and type the amount. It is not an expense.
4. To check, count the box, tap [Count cash], type the amount and [Save count].
5. Short or over? The difference is kept with the count. Look for a cash expense not recorded yet.

Tip: Every cash expense lowers what should be there. The same line is on Month end.
