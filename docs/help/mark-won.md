1. On the RFQ, tap the green [Mark won]. It turns green once the quotation is out.
2. Each item shows its best-ranked supplier. Change one only if needed.
3. Tick the lines you are selling. Each price comes from your latest quotation. No quotation yet? Type the price from your costing sheet.
4. Type the [Customer PO number] if you have it, and check [Payment terms in days].
5. Tap [Create order]. The order opens.

Tip: The customer said no? Tap [Mark lost] instead and pick the reason. Every order starts from a won RFQ. The customer ordering the same again? Use [Repeat a past order].

Safe to try: pressing [Create order] twice never makes two orders.
