1. On the RFQ, select the green [Mark won]. It turns green once the quotation is out.
2. Pick the supplier for each item from its supplier prices. Nothing is chosen for you.
3. Tick the lines you are selling. Each price comes from your latest quotation. With no quotation yet, type the price from your costing sheet.
4. Type the [Customer PO number] if you have it, and check [Payment terms in days].
5. Select [Create order]. The order opens.

Tip: Every order starts from a won RFQ. When the customer orders the same again, use [Repeat a past order].

Safe to try: pressing [Create order] twice never makes two orders.
