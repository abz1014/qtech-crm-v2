1. Open the job and its Order stage. The Customer PO line shows the PO number.
2. Tap [Attach PO file]. Choose the PDF or a photo of the PO. You can choose more than one.
3. Each file shows as "PO file:" with its name. Tap the name to open or save it.
4. The file is also listed in [Notes and files], tagged Customer PO.

Tip: Engineers never see the PO file, even a photo of it. It is kept private for you and the admin.
