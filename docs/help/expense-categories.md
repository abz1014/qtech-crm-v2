1. Select the avatar at the top, then [Settings], then [Expense categories].
2. To add a category, select [Add category], then choose its group and how it is usually paid.
3. To change a category, open its ⋯ menu: [Rename], [Move to group…] or [Set monthly budget…].
4. To combine two categories for the same thing, open the ⋯ menu of one, select [Merge into…] and choose the other. Its expenses move across.
5. To retire a category, select [Archive]. Existing expenses keep it. New expenses cannot use it.

Tip: The group decides where the spending shows in Profit and loss. A category with expenses in a locked month can only move to a group of the same kind.
