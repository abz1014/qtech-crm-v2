1. Tap [Money], then [Reports], then [Expenses]. Or tap [Report for <month>] on the Expenses list.
2. Tap [Month] or [Quarter], then use the arrows to change the period.
3. Read the total, the change from last period and the cash and bank split.
4. Open a category to see its details, like each car's fuel, and every entry.
5. Tap [By detail] to see details across all categories.
6. Tap [Export CSV] for the figures or [Download PDF] to print.

Tip: The total matches In vs out's money out. Running costs and order costs match Profit and loss.
