1. Open the job from [To do] or [Jobs]. When the goods are here its green next step is [Deliver the goods].
2. Under [Supplier orders], find the supplier.
3. Select [Delivered today]. One select: the date is today's.
4. If it was delivered on another day, select the date and change it.

Note: Engineers who ordered from the supplier themselves select [I placed this order] on that supplier order.

Note: To reverse a wrong selection, select [Undo] on the message at the bottom.
