1. Tap [Money], then [GST to FBR] under Accounts.
2. Each month shows GST charged, less GST paid on purchases. What is left is [To pay].
3. Pay by the 15th of the next month and file the return by the 18th. A month turns amber after the 12th and red after the 15th.
4. When it is paid, the month says Paid, with the date and the payment slip number (PSID).

Tip: The PSID and the deposit date are stamped on the invoices in [Invoices], a month at a time.
