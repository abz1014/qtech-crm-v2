1. Open [Settings] from the menu under your picture. The version is at the bottom, like "Version a1b2c3d · 26 Oct 2026".
2. Tap [Check for update now].
3. It says "You have the latest version." or switches to the newest one by itself.

The app also updates by itself when you come back to it, as long as you are not in the middle of typing. If it shows [Reload], tap it when you are ready: what you typed is kept.
