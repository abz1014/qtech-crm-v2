1. From the 1st to the 10th, [To do] has a month-closing row. Tap [Close month] and work through its checks.
2. From the 1st until it is paid, a [GST to FBR] row shows last month's GST. It is grey to the 12th, amber from the 13th, red after the 15th.
3. Pay by the 15th and file the return by the 18th. Tap [Open GST], deposit the tax and record the payment slip number.
4. When an engineer has paid for something, an [I paid: N to approve] row appears. Tap [Approve] to open the claims.

Tip: These rows are for the admin only. The GST row says how many invoices are unpaid, not the amount: the amount is on the Money screen.
