1. Open the job. The green button says the next step. Tap it: that stage opens right here.
2. Supplier order: for a line with no supplier tap [Choose supplier], then [Issue PO] for each supplier.
3. Delivery: tap [Delivered today] when the goods arrive. Late? Tap [Follow up on delivery].
4. Invoice: [Create invoice] shows once all goods are delivered. Then [Record payment].
5. To delete a mistaken order, tap [More], then [Delete order]. Only an admin sees it.

Tip: Engineers see the goods and the installation, never prices or invoices.
