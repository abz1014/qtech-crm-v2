title: Close an RFQ that was lost
roles: sales, admin
keywords: close, lost, no reply, cancel, reason, dead, reopen
screens: /rfqs, /todo, /jobs
---
1. Open the RFQ. Quotation already sent? Tap [Mark lost] beside [Mark won]. Otherwise tap [More].
2. Pick the reason (in [More] it is under [Close the RFQ because]).
3. It leaves your open RFQs. Reports use the reason.

Tip: On [To do], a quotation with no decision has [Lost on price] and [Lost to a competitor] under it. No need to open the RFQ.

Safe to try: closed by mistake? Tap [Undo] on the message at the bottom, or later [Reopen] on the RFQ.
