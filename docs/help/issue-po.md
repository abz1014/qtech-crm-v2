1. Open the order. While a supplier is not ordered from yet, the green button is [Issue PO].
2. Check the prices, the [Currency] and the terms. Tap [Preview PDF] to see it.
3. Tap [Issue PO], then send the PDF to the supplier.
4. Ordered some other way? Tap [Mark placed] instead.

Tip: Issuing marks the supplier order placed today. Today then reminds you when the goods are late.
