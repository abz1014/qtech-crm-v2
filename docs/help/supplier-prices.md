1. Open the RFQ. In [Suppliers asked], tap [Enter reply] beside the supplier.
2. Tap [Paste reply], paste what the supplier sent, then tap [Read it]. A PDF quote, or a screenshot of one? Tap [Read a PDF or photo].
3. Check the price, currency and lead time it read.
4. Tap [Accept N prices].
5. Or type each price in its box and tap [Save reply].
6. The supplier has no price? Tap [Can't quote] and pick the reason.

Tip: Prices in other currencies are shown in PKR at today's rate, with their own figure in grey below. Suppliers are listed by name: the CRM never marks or suggests one. To see everything side by side, or to make a PDF, tap [Open the comparative statement].

Safe to try: nothing is saved until you accept or save.
