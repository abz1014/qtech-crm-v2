1. On the Jobs list, type in [Search jobs]: a job number ("Job 139"), an RFQ, order, quotation (QT-), PO or invoice number, a customer or an item.
2. Tap the job to open it.
3. Press Ctrl K from any screen and type the same words: the Jobs group shows the same jobs.

Tip: A search looks in every list, so a finished job is found too. The Mine, Open and History buttons only choose what the list shows when the search box is empty. Under them, Show jobs at stage narrows the list to jobs waiting at one step, such as To invoice (the office) or Waiting for supplier.

Each job on a phone is a card of five short rows: the customer and the job number; the customer's PO (the office only) with a small Imported tag on a job that came from the old system; the items in one line ("3 items · Flow meter"); where the job stands in words ("Stage 4 of 7 · Supplier order") over a thin bar; and the one next step to take.
