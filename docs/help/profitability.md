1. Tap [Money], then [Profitability].
2. Read [Average monthly income]. It is what we invoiced, GST left out, over the last 12 complete months.
3. Beside it, [Average cash received a month] is what customers paid, GST included.
4. Tap [12 months] or [24 months] to change the chart. Tap [Show as a table] for the exact figures.
5. Tap [See the profit of each order], then [Cash] to see what each order received and paid out.

Tip: This month is left out of the average because it is not over. A month with no invoices counts as zero.
