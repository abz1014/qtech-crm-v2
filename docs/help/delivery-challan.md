1. Open the job. Admin and sales: select Delivery, then the Challans row. Engineers find Delivery challans on their job page.
2. Select [New challan]. Every goods line is ticked at its full quantity.
3. For a part delivery, untick a line or lower its quantity.
4. Check where it goes, then select [Issue and download]. The challan gets the next DC number and downloads as a PDF.

Note: A challan lists goods and quantities only, never prices, and has a line for the person who receives them to sign.
