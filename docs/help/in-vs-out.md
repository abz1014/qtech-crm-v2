1. Tap [Money], then [Reports], then [In vs out].
2. Tap [Month] or [Quarter], then use the arrows to change the period.
3. Cash in vs out is the money that actually moved. Profit (invoiced basis) counts sales on their invoice date, without GST.
4. Read the box under them: it says why profit and cash differ.
5. Under money out by category, each category lists its details, such as each car's fuel.
6. Tap [Export CSV] for the exact figures, with paisa.

Tip: A closed month shows the figures frozen when it was closed. Quarters are calendar quarters: Q1 is January to March.
