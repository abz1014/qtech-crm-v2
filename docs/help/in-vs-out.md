1. Tap [Money], then [Reports].
2. In [Report], pick [Money in and out].
3. Tap [Month] or [Quarter], then use the arrows to change the period.
4. [Cash in minus cash out] is the money that actually moved. [Profit on invoices] counts sales on their invoice date, without GST.
5. Read the box under them: it says why profit and cash differ.
6. Tap [Export CSV] for the exact figures, with paisa.

Tip: Profit and loss shows profit on money received instead; [Why two profits?] explains the difference. Quarters are calendar quarters: Q1 is January to March.
