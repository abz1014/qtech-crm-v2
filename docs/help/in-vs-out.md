1. Select [Money], then [Reports].
2. In [Report], choose [Money in and out].
3. Select [Month] or [Quarter], then use the arrows to change the period.
4. [Cash in minus cash out] is the money that actually moved. [Profit on invoices] counts sales on their invoice date, without GST.
5. The box under them explains why profit and cash differ.
6. Select [Export CSV] for the exact figures, with paisa.

Note: Profit and loss shows profit on money received instead; [Profit on invoices and profit on cash] explains the difference. Quarters are calendar quarters: Q1 is January to March.
