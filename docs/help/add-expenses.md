1. Tap [Money], then [Expenses], then [Add expenses]. Or press Ctrl+K and type "add expense".
2. Type the amount, press Enter on a phone or Tab on a computer, then type the category.
3. No such category yet? Keep typing its name and tap [Create “<name>”], paid in cash or by bank.
4. Optional: a [Detail], like the car "LEA-1234" or "Office water". Details you typed before are suggested.
5. Tap [Save N expenses], or press Ctrl+Enter. All rows save together.

Tip: No receipt or supplier is needed for a small spend. On a phone the Cash / Bank chip changes with one tap, and the camera button adds a receipt photo.
