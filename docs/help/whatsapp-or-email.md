1. Every message the app writes shows [WhatsApp] and [Email] above it.
2. Pick one once. The app remembers it on this device.
3. Copy the message with the button beside it.
4. Paste it into WhatsApp or your email and press Send yourself.

Tip: The app never sends anything on its own and keeps no copy of the message.
