title: Hide an item on Today until later
roles: sales, admin
keywords: snooze, later, hide, not now, remind me, tomorrow

---
1. On [Today], tap the row.
2. Tap [Snooze].
3. Pick [Tomorrow morning], [Monday] or [Pick a date].
4. It comes back on that day. Only you stop seeing it.

Safe to try: tap [Undo] on the message at the bottom to put it back.
