1. Select the avatar at the top, then [Settings], then [Exchange rates].
2. On the row of the currency, select [Add rate]. The form below selects that currency and places the cursor in the rate.
3. Enter the rate in PKR for 1 unit, for example the TT rate of the bank.
4. Select [Save rate].

Tip: A manual rate wins for its own day. Select [History] on a row to see the recent rates of that currency.
