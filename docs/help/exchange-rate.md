1. Tap your avatar at the top, then [Settings], then [Exchange rates].
2. On the currency's row, tap [Add rate]. The form below picks that currency and puts the cursor in the rate.
3. Type the rate in PKR for 1 unit, for example the bank's TT rate.
4. Tap [Save rate].

Tip: A rate entered by hand wins for its own day. Tap [History] on a row to see that currency's recent rates.
