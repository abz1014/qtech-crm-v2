1. Open the job from the Jobs list. The top block shows a bar of seven parts and a line such as "Stage 4 of 7".
2. The steps are one list: Enquiry, Quotation, Order, Supplier order, Delivery, Invoice, Payment. A tick is done, a filled dot is now, a dashed circle is not yet.
3. The one green button is in the top block, or in the bar at the bottom of a phone. It opens the step where you do it.
4. Tap a step to open it. Contact, Money (PKR), Add to this job and Notes and files are rows in the same list.

Tip: Engineers see Invoice and Payment as "With the office". A job that started as an order shows no Enquiry or Quotation.

Under the job's name, three lines are always shown once there is an order: the customer's PO (admin and sales only), the supplier, and where the goods are (ordered, how many are delivered).

Jobs that came from the old system may have an "Imported note" row at the end of the list (admin and sales only). It is closed; tap it to read the old notes and the item lines as they were imported. They can name suppliers and prices, so engineers never see them, and an imported job's item text shows only the first item and "+N more".
