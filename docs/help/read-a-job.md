1. Open the job from the Jobs list. The ruler shows seven stops: Enquiry, Quotation, Order, Supplier order, Delivery, Invoice, Payment.
2. The dark number is where it stands now; ticks are done. A note under a stop is something running beside it, such as "2 of 3 delivered".
3. Read the Next step band. Its one green button opens the page where you do it.
4. Tap a stop, or a row under the ruler, to open that stage and see what is in it.

Tip: Engineers see Invoice and Payment as "Handled by the office". A job that started as an order shows no Enquiry or Quotation.
