1. On Monday, tap the [Weekly review] row on [To do]. It is also always under the [Mine] and [Team] switch.
2. Read the four lists: quotations with no answer, enquiries nobody asked suppliers about, goods far late, payments far late.
3. For each row tap [Still live], [Chase] or [Close as lost].
4. To decide several at once, tick them, then tap [Still live] or [Close selected as lost] in the bar.
5. Wrong tap? Tap [Undo] on the message at the bottom.

Tip: [Still live] keeps a row out of the next two reviews. Late payments also stay at the top of [To do].
