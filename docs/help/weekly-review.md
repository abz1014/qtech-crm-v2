1. On Monday, select the [Weekly review] row on [To do]. It is also always under the [Mine] and [Team] switch.
2. Read the four lists: quotations with no answer, RFQs with no supplier asked, goods far late, payments far late.
3. For each row select [Still live], [Follow up] or [Close as lost].
4. To decide several at once, tick them, then select [Still live] or [Close selected as lost] in the bar.
5. To reverse a choice, select [Undo] on the message at the bottom.

Tip: [Still live] keeps a row out of the next two reviews. Late payments also stay at the top of [To do].
