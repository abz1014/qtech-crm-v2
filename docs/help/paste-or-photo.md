1. Open the job, then the row [Add to this job]. Paste the message under [Paste here], or select [Take a photo] or [Choose a file].
2. It says what it looks like: a supplier price, the customer's answer, a site photo or a note. If the type is wrong, select the right one.
3. A supplier's prices: select [Accept N prices]. The prices go on that supplier's row.
4. A photo or a note: select [Add photo] or [Add note]. There are no other fields.
5. To share from WhatsApp on a phone: Share to Q-Tech CRM, then pick [New RFQ] or [Add to job…].

Tip: An engineer can add a photo or a note. Prices are for sales and the owner.

Safe to try: if it was added to the wrong job, select Undo on the message at the bottom.
