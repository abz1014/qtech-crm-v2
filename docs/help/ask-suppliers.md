1. After [Looks right: find suppliers], the usual suppliers are already ticked. Tick or untick any.
2. Tap [Write the message] and check it.
3. Tap [Copy message for N suppliers].
4. Under [Send it to each supplier], tap [Open in WhatsApp] for each one and press Send there.
5. When it asks [Did you send it?], tap [Yes, sent]. Tap [Not yet] if you did not. Until you say yes, the RFQ shows Asked, never Sent.
6. Later, to ask more: open the RFQ and tap [Ask more suppliers].

Tip: An item with no supplier ticked is named under the list; tap [Tick] to add the supplier that carries its brand. On an RFQ nobody has been asked about yet, the green button is [Ask suppliers].

Safe to try: wrong supplier? Tap [Undo] on the message at the bottom.
