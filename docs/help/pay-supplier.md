1. Open the order. Under [Supplier orders], find the supplier.
2. Open its menu and tap [Pay <supplier>]. The payee, the order and what is still owed are filled in.
3. Check the amount and [Paid via].
4. Tap [Save expense].

Tip: What is still owed to suppliers is in [We owe suppliers] under Reports.
