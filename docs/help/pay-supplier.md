1. Open the order. Under [Supplier orders], open the supplier's menu and select [Pay <supplier>].
2. A foreign supplier: enter [Paid in <currency>] and the [PKR debited from the bank].
3. A local supplier: check the [Amount, PKR]. The balance due is filled in.
4. Bank charges go in [Bank charges, PKR]. They are saved as their own Bank charges expense.
5. Select [Save payment].

Note: This is the only way to record a supplier payment, so Payable to suppliers always goes down. [Payable to suppliers] under Reports has [Pay] on each row, which opens the same form.
