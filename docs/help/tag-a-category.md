1. On an RFQ, tap the category chip under an item. A dashed chip is only a guess: tap it and choose the category to save it.
2. On a supplier's page, find [Categories they deal in]. Tap [Tag] on a category they supply, or [Untag] to take it off.
3. To tag one that is not listed, use [Tag a category] under the list.

Tip: a supplier that quotes an item in a category is added to it by itself. Tagged suppliers are listed under "Deals in" when you ask for prices.
