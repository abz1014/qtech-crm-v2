1. Tap [Money], then [Expenses]. In [Quick add] type the amount.
2. Tap [Other]. Or type what it was, and tap the Other offered under it.
3. Write [What was it for?], for example "Courier from shop to office". It cannot be left empty.
4. Tap [Save]. The expense report lists each Other by what you wrote.
5. When one title comes back 3 times, a banner asks if it should be a category. Tap [Make it a category].

Tip: Other keeps the category list short. Each group has its own Other, picked from the words you type.
