1. Tap your avatar at the top, then [Settings], then [Expense categories].
2. To add one, tap [Add category], type the name, pick the group and how it is usually paid, then tap [Add category] in the box.
3. To change one, open its row menu and tap [Edit name, group or paid via].
4. To stop offering one for new expenses, open its row menu and tap [Deactivate]. Old expenses keep it.

Tip: A category nothing uses has a [Delete] button on its row. One in use can only be deactivated.
