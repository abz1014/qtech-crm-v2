1. At the bottom of the screen there are [Home], [Camera], [I paid] and [Jobs].
2. Select [Camera], pick the job, then add the photo or the note on its page.
3. To claim money spent on the job, select [I paid], type the amount, take a photo of the receipt and save. The office approves it.

Note: Money pages are for the office. The same photo and note button is in the [Add to this job] row of each job.
