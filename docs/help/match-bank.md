1. Tap [Money], then [Month end], then [Match bank statement].
2. Tap each line that is on your bank statement. A whole PSID group is one tap.
3. Compare [Ticked, money in] and [Ticked, money out] with the statement's totals.
4. It is done when it says [Everything ticked].

Tip: Cash payments and expenses never appear here.
