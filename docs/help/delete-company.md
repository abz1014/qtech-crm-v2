1. Open the company and tap [More], then [Delete company]. Only an admin sees it.
2. If it says the company is used, tap [Merge into…] and choose the company that should keep everything.
3. If it is not used, tap [Delete]. This cannot be undone.

Tip: A company with RFQs, orders, invoices or expenses is merged, never deleted.
