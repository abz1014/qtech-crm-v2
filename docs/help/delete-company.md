roles: admin

---
1. Open the company and select [More], then [Delete company]. Only an admin sees it.
2. If the company is in use, select [Merge into…] and choose the company that should keep everything.
3. If the company is not in use, select [Delete]. This cannot be undone.

Tip: A company with RFQs, orders, invoices or expenses is merged, never deleted.
