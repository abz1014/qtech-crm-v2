1. Select [Money], then [Expenses]. Engineers' claims are under [To approve].
2. Open the [Receipt] if there is one.
3. Pick the [Category] and how it was [Paid back]: cash given back, from petty cash, or bank transfer.
4. Select [Approve]. It becomes an expense and counts from then on.
5. To reject a claim, select [Not approved…], enter the reason, then [Not approved].

Note: A claim shows its job and the visit the engineer named. The expense keeps both, so an RFQ's cost joins its order when it is won. A claim waiting here is in no total or report. The engineer can see whether it was approved.
