1. Tap [Money], then [Month end]. Today reminds you in the first 10 days of the month.
2. Only the checks with something to look at are listed. Tap one to fix it.
3. None of them blocks. Tap [Close <month>], then confirm.
4. Closed by mistake? Tap [Reopen] under [Closed months]. It is logged.
5. Only the latest closed month has [Reopen]. To reopen an earlier month, reopen the newer ones first, newest first.

Tip: Closing freezes payments, expenses and invoices dated in that month.
