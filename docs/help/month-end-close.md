1. Tap [Money], then [Lock a month]. Today reminds you in the first 10 days of the month.
2. Only the checks with something to look at are listed. Tap one to fix it.
3. None of them blocks. Tap [Close <month>], then confirm. One tap closes one month only.
4. Months close one at a time, oldest first, and only while that month is on screen.
5. If an older month is still open, the green button says Go to that month. It only moves you there and closes nothing. Then tap [Close <month>].
6. Closed by mistake? Tap [Reopen] under [Closed months]. It is logged.
7. Only the latest closed month has [Reopen]. To reopen an earlier month, reopen the newer ones first, newest first.

Tip: Closing freezes payments, expenses and invoices dated in that month.
