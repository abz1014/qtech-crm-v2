1. Tap [To do], then [Team].
2. Each person has a panel. The line under the name says how many of their items are late.
3. Late rows are red, due today rows are amber. Work older than 30 days is grey under [Older than 30 days], with its date.
4. Tap [Mine] to go back to your own list.

Tip: This view is read only. Each person works their own list.
