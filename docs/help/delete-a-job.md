1. Open the job and tap [More], then [Delete job]. Only an admin sees it.
2. If nothing is in the way, read what goes with it, then tap [Delete]. This cannot be undone.
3. If it says "This job cannot be deleted yet", do the steps under "To do first" in order. Each has a link to the place.
4. Tap [Check again]. When nothing is left, the same window asks you to delete.
5. Invoices with a number and voided payments stay for the books, so the window says "This job cannot be deleted" (no "yet"). Tap [Mark the job cancelled].

Tip: Cancelling keeps the job and its money records. Deleting removes the job and its orders for good.
