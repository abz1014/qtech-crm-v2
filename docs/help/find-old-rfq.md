1. Tap [Search] at the top. On a computer, Ctrl K opens it too.
2. Type an RFQ or order number, the customer's reference, a company or an item.
3. Pick it from the list.

Tip: Closed RFQs and finished orders are found too. Search also finds Settings pages by name.
