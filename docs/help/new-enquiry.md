1. Tap [New], then [New enquiry]. Or drop the customer's PDF, photo or spreadsheet anywhere on To do or on Jobs.
2. The document is on the left and what was read is on the right. Look at anything marked [Check this].
3. Tap [Use <company>] when the customer is suggested, or choose the company.
4. Tap [Create job]. The job opens. Nobody is asked for a price yet.
5. On the job, tap Ask suppliers when you are ready.

Tip: You can also paste the message anywhere on the page. A paste, a PDF, a photo and a spreadsheet are read the same way, on this device.
