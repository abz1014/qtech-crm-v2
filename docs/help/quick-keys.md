1. Press N for a new enquiry, E for an expense (admin) or O for an order without an enquiry. They work when the cursor is not in a text box.
2. Or tap [New] at the top and pick one.
3. For an expense, [Add an expense] opens: type the amount, tap what it was for, then tap [Save]. It stays open for the next one.
4. Text too small? Open your initials, then [Larger] under [Text size].

Tip: Single keys can be turned off in Settings, Your details.
