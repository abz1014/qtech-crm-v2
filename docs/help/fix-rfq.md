1. Open the RFQ and tap [More], then [Edit RFQ details].
2. Change the customer, site, contact, customer ref or received date, then tap [Save changes].
3. To fix an item, tap the three dots on the item, then [Edit item] and [Save item]. To drop it, tap [Remove item].

Tip: You can change what you entered. If it says "Entered by" someone else, ask them or an admin. An item an order is based on stays until the order line is changed.
