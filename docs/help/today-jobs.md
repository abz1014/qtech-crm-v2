1. Select [Home]. It lists your jobs: orders you are an engineer on, and the goods on your own orders.
2. "Commissioning on" a day means a site visit in the next 5 working days.
3. Red means today or late.
4. Select a row, then [Open <order number>] to see the site, the contact and the notes.

Tip: Money pages are for the office. That is normal.
