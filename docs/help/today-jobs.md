1. Tap [Today]. It lists your jobs: orders you are an engineer on, and the goods on your own orders.
2. "Commissioning on" a day means a site visit in the next 5 working days.
3. Red means today or late.
4. Tap a row, then [Open <order number>] to see the site, the contact and the notes.

Tip: You do not see payments, costs or margins. That is normal.
