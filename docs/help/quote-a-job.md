1. Open the job. The top block says what is due: select its green button, for example [Send quotation].
2. Select [Issue quotation], type a price for each item and send the PDF.
3. For a quotation made outside the CRM, select [Attach our quotation] in Quotations. It is marked sent.
4. Asking suppliers in the CRM is optional. Save their prices and emails as files in Notes and files if you like.
5. When the customer orders, select [Mark won] and confirm the lines: the order starts and the job moves on.

Tip: Engineers do not see prices, quotations or Mark won.
