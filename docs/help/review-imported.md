1. Open Review imported. It lists imported orders and invoices that still show money or goods open.
2. Tap [Find matches]. It proposes which order each invoice belongs to. Nothing is linked yet.
3. Tap [Link all certain] for the sure ones. For a likely one, check the reasons, then tap [Accept] or [Not this].
4. For each record tap [Still open] (it stays on To do) or [Settled, keep as history].
5. The count at the top says how many are decided.

Tip: Settled keeps the record for reports and searching, but it stops counting as money owed.
