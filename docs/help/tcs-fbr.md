1. Tap [Money], then [Invoices & GST].
2. Tick the invoices the courier took, then tap [Sent to customer today].
3. Tax deposited? Tick them, tap [Add PSID], paste the PSID once, then tap [Deposited today].
4. FBR receipt: open the invoice and tap [Add receipt].

Tip: [Needs attention] shows what is late.

Safe to try: stamped by mistake? Tap [Undo] on the message at the bottom.
