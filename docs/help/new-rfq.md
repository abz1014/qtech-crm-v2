1. Tap [New], then [New enquiry]. (On the old RFQ list the button is [New RFQ].)
2. Paste what the customer sent, or tap [Choose PDF or photo] for their PDF, a screenshot, a photo or an Excel sheet (.xlsx, .xls, .csv). It splits into items.
3. Under [Customer], tap [Use <Company>] if it is suggested, or pick the company.
4. Check the items, then tap [Looks right: find suppliers].
5. Next, ask suppliers for prices.

Tip: The reference and due date are filled in from the PDF when it has them. More to add after the split? Tap [Paste more]. Not ready to ask suppliers? Tap [Save for later (don't ask suppliers yet)]; on a phone it is under the ⋯ button. Left halfway? Open [New enquiry] again and tap [Continue your draft]. A message or photo from WhatsApp can also start with [New enquiry].

Safe to try: nothing is saved until you copy the supplier message or save.
