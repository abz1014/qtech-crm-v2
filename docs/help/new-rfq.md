1. Tap [RFQs], then [New RFQ].
2. Under [Customer], pick the company. Type their [Customer reference] if they gave one.
3. Paste what the customer sent. It splits into items. Blank spec lines are fine.
4. Check the items, then tap [Looks right: find suppliers].
5. Next, ask suppliers for prices.

Tip: Left halfway? Open [New RFQ] again and tap [Continue your draft]. A message or photo from WhatsApp can also start with [Paste or photo].

Safe to try: nothing is saved until you copy the supplier message or save.
