1. Tap [Money]. It opens on [Who owes us].
2. Tap an overdue order. The reminder is written under [Payment reminder].
3. Pick [WhatsApp] or [Email].
4. Tap [Copy and mark reminded], paste it to the customer and send.

Tip: Overdue payments also show on Today. Payments are recorded by the admin.

Safe to try: reminded by mistake? Tap [Undo] on the message at the bottom.
