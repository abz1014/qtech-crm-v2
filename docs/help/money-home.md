1. Select [Money]. Four blocks show this month so far.
2. [To collect] is the amount customers still have to pay. The red part is already past its due date. Select it to see which customers.
3. Received and Paid out are cash this month, by the day it moved. Select one to see where it came from.
4. [Payable to suppliers] is what is left to pay on supplier orders. Select it, then use Pay on the order.
5. "Cash, not profit" shows how much of the amount received is GST payable to FBR. The profit of last month is one selection away.

Tip: [Add expense] is the one green button. Bank check, GST to FBR and Lock a month are under [Accounts], one level down. On every other Money page, the row [More money pages] under the tabs (on a phone, [All Money pages]) links to Margins, Mistakes to fix, Bank check, Review imported and GST to FBR.
