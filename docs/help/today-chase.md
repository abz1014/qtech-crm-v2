1. Tap [To do]. Read [Late] first, then [Today].
2. On a row, tap [Chase]. The message is already written under [Prepared for you].
3. The channel you used last is chosen. To switch, tap [Email instead] or [WhatsApp instead].
4. Tap [Open in WhatsApp] or [Open in email], press Send there, then tap [Mark chased].
5. The row moves to [Chased, waiting] and comes back on its date if nobody answers.

Tip: [Copy and mark chased] copies and marks in one tap. [This week] holds what is not due yet.

Safe to try: chased by mistake? Tap [Undo] on the message at the bottom.
