1. Tap [Today]. Read [Overdue or due today] first.
2. Tap a row. The message is already written under [Prepared for you].
3. Pick [WhatsApp] or [Email]. The app remembers your choice.
4. Tap [Copy message and mark chased], then paste it into the chat and send.
5. The row moves to [Waiting] and comes back on its date if nobody answers.

Tip: On a phone the button reads [Copy and mark chased]. The tabs [Quotations] and [Suppliers & delivery] show one kind of work.

Safe to try: chased by mistake? Tap [Undo] on the message at the bottom.
