Capture sheet

Gate 2, D12. One sheet for everything a team member receives on WhatsApp, on Android, iPhone and desktop. It takes the text and photos, asks one question (where does this go?), and saves. Friday 27 November 2026. The WhatsApp and system screens are plain stand-ins; they are not Q-Tech screens.

Android: Share to Q-Tech CRM

The installed web app registers as a share target, so it appears in WhatsApp’s share sheet. The shared text and photo arrive in the Capture sheet.

iPhone: copy, open, paste

Safari cannot add Q-Tech CRM to a share sheet, so the first step is Copy. From the Capture button on, the sheet is the same one Android uses, with the same destinations and the same Save.

Tap count

A tap is one deliberate touch. Press and hold followed by a menu choice counts as one, because the menu opens under the same finger. Switching apps counts as one.

Rules the design follows

  • Android best case is 3 taps. iPhone best case is 5, which is 2 more.
  • Every destination path ends within 3 taps once the sheet is open: choose an action, choose an RFQ or question, then Save.
  • The common case, answering the questions on the RFQ you opened most recently, needs only Save.
  • A photo and its text are two copies on iPhone. Paste twice, or use Choose photos. On Android a photo with a caption arrives as one share.

The four destinations

The chooser is two short questions on one screen: what to do, and on which RFQ. Recents are the RFQs opened in the last 24 hours plus your own RFQs with open questions, most recent first.

Signed out in the middle

A shared item is saved on the device before the app checks the session, so a login redirect cannot lose it. It is kept for 7 days, then removed.

Desktop

WhatsApp Desktop users copy or drag from the chat. The Capture button is in the top bar and C opens it from anywhere. Dropping a file on the window opens the sheet with it already added.

Nothing added yet

Desktop 1440. Paste works anywhere in the sheet; the choice on the right wakes up when something is added.
