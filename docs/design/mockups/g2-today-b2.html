Waiting group

The system's cooldown, not the user's snooze. Dimmed, at the foot of the list, never in the count of things that need you.

Rules this frame follows

  • Row text reads "chased 2 days ago · next nudge on Thu 3 Dec". The date is the day the item returns.
  • 5 items or fewer: open. More than 5: collapsed, and the choice to open is the user's. Opening is remembered only for the session.
  • Snoozed items are not here at all. A snooze is the user's choice, Waiting is the system's.
  • The wait is shorter when the bid is close: RFQ-0177 returns on Mon 30 Nov, 1 working day, because the bid is due Tue 1 Dec.
  • Dimmed with colour, not opacity: every line stays above 4.5:1.
