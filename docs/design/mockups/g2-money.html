Money: the GST register, what customers owe, and cash in and out

Three clocks, never combined. The register follows the invoice date, the cash month follows the payment date, and margin follows the life of the order. Every figure is net, GST and gross, and every table adds up to the line beneath it. The sales view keeps invoices, the register and per-order balances, and drops cash, costs, margin and rates.
