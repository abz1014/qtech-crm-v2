Form

Validation sits beside the field and is summarised once at the top. A unique violation names the existing record. A form never submits twice.

Dialog

Modal on desktop, bottom sheet on phones. Focus moves in on open, stays inside, and returns to the button that opened it.

Anatomy

  • Width 400, 480 or 640. Radius 4, shadow-modal over the 42 percent scrim.
  • Header: 36 px tone tile, 16 px semibold title, 13 px muted line, 28 px close.
  • Footer: sunken band, buttons right, optional lead text left.

Keyboard

  • Open: focus goes to the first field, or to the least destructive button on a confirm.
  • Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside. Esc closes unless busy.
  • Enter submits when focus is in a field. Close returns focus to the opener.

Motion

  • Fades in and rises 6 px over 200 ms, ease-out.
  • The scrim fades only.
  • Reduced motion: no movement, instant.

Rows

  • Hover: bg-sunken. Selected: bg-selected plus a 3 px brand-green edge. Keyboard focus: 2 px brand-green ring, inset.
  • Whole row is one target when it opens something; row actions stop propagation.
  • Touch: rows are 48 px.

Columns

  • Text left, numbers and dates right or mono, status chips left.
  • Header 36 px, 12 px medium, muted. Sortable headers are buttons with aria-sort.
  • Long text truncates with an ellipsis and a tooltip, never wraps in a row.

Keyboard

  • ↑ ↓ or J K move the focus row, Enter opens it, Space selects it.
  • / focuses the filter. Esc clears it.
  • Focus is remembered when you come back from a detail view.

Empty state

Four reasons a list can be empty, and what each should say.

Pattern

  • A 40 px icon tile in a tone, a 16 px title, one or two lines of muted text, then at most two actions.
  • The title states what is true. The line below says what happens next or what to do.
  • The goal state uses success. First use and no results stay neutral. Missing access uses warning.
  • Never an illustration beyond the tile, never a joke.

Where it lives

  • Inside the container that would have held the list: a table body, a list pane or a panel.
  • Keeps the toolbar, so the filter can still be cleared.
  • Today’s goal state is the same pattern at page width, with “ahead of you” rows underneath.

Tooltip

Short, optional, never the only place something is said.

Rules

  • Ink tone, shadow-pop, 12 px, 6 px by 8 px padding, 4 px radius, no arrow.
  • Opens after 400 ms on hover, immediately on focus. Closes on Esc, blur or leaving.
  • Never holds the only copy of important information. Phones do not show tooltips; use visible text.
