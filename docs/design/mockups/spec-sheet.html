Principles

An instrument panel, tidied. The restraint is the design.

Hairlines before shadowsSurfaces are flat. A 1px neutral line separates; only menus and dialogs float.
Green means one of three thingsPrimary action, where you are, or focus. Success is teal, so the two never get confused.
Numbers are set like a ledgerPKR, RFQ and PO numbers, part codes and dates use a mono face with tabular figures.
Colour is never aloneEvery status has a glyph shape and a word. Dashed outline means “not firm”.

Motion

Transform and opacity only, in answer to an action. One slow reveal per view at most. Hover a row to play it.

  • Menus fade and rise 4px (--dur-base). Panels fade and rise 6px (--dur-slow).
  • Date rails draw once from the left. Skeletons pulse opacity 1 to 0.45.
  • No bounce, no spring, no scroll-triggered or staggered entrances.
  • prefers-reduced-motion collapses every duration to 0.01 ms and stops loops.
