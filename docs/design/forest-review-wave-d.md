## First impression

Round 4 finished the last screens in the Forest look, and each lane matched its mockup on its own page. The seams show where the new
screens meet the shell and the shared parts: Settings keeps its list beside the page down to 768, so on a tablet or a small laptop the
page gets a 288 to 376 px column and Team breaks names and emails into three-letter lines; the search palette's key legend wraps at
1440 and leaves a blank grey band on a phone, with no way to close it but a sliver of scrim; the History panel still covers the record
it describes; the Keyboard shortcuts sheet lays every key under its action at every width (a breakpoint Tailwind never generated);
Help's on-screen label chips break mid-label; and a few dialogs and lists put the same button in two places on one screen.
