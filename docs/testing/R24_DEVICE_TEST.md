# R24: phone test, about 15 minutes

For the owner. No technical knowledge needed. You need: your Android phone (Chrome), your iPhone (Safari), WhatsApp on the Android phone,
a Q-Tech login, and one RFQ and one order you can safely add a test note to (use a test one, not a customer's live one).

How to read this: do the steps in order. Tick the box when what you see matches. If it does not match, take a screenshot
(Android: Power + Volume Down; iPhone: Side button + Volume Up), write the step number in the message, and send it. Then carry on with the next step.

Count your taps in Part A step A8 and Part B step B6 (a tap is one touch of the screen). We only need the two numbers.

## Part A: Android phone, Chrome (about 8 minutes)

**A1. Open the app.** Open Chrome. Type `qtechcrm.vercel.app` and go. Sign in with your Q-Tech login.
- You should see the Q-Tech CRM home screen with a green **Capture** button at the top. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**A2. Install it.** Tap your round account picture or name, then tap **Install app**. (If Chrome shows its own "Install app" box instead, tap Install.) Tap **Install** again if asked.
- You should see a **Q-Tech** icon appear on your home screen. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**A3. Open it from the icon.** Close Chrome. Tap the **Q-Tech** icon.
- It opens full screen with no Chrome address bar, and you are still signed in. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**A4. Share a WhatsApp message.** Open WhatsApp, press and hold any customer message with a few lines of text, tap the **Share/Forward** arrow, choose **Share** (the "..." or Android share menu), then choose **Q-Tech** in the list of apps.
- Q-Tech opens on a **Capture** sheet with the text already in it, and a small line saying **Shared from WhatsApp**. [ ]
- If **Q-Tech** is not in the share list, screenshot the share list and send. [ ]

**A5. Attach the text to an RFQ.** On the Capture sheet, under "What should we do with it?", tap **Add to RFQ notes**. Under "On which RFQ", tap your test RFQ. Tap the green **Add to notes** button at the bottom.
- You should see a message **Saved to** and the RFQ number, with **Open** and **Capture another** buttons. [ ]
- Tap **Open** and check the text is in the RFQ's notes. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**A6. Share a photo.** Open the Photos or Gallery app, open any photo, tap **Share**, choose **Q-Tech**.
- Capture opens and shows the photo under **What you captured**, with a short "Resizing" bar that finishes. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**A7. Attach the photo to an RFQ.** Tap **Add to RFQ notes**, pick your test RFQ, tap **Add to notes** (if it says **Save photo as the answer** you chose the wrong tile, tap **Add to RFQ notes** instead).
- You see **Saved to** and the RFQ number. Tap **Open**: the photo is attached to the RFQ. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**A8. Attach a photo to an order.** Share another photo to **Q-Tech** (as in A6). On the Capture sheet tap the small underlined words **Attach to an order**. Under "On which order" tap your test order, then tap **Attach to order**. Count the taps from the moment you pressed Share on the photo until you saw "Saved to": ______ taps.
- You see **Saved to** and the order number; **Open** shows the photo on the order. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**A9. Offline note.** Turn on Airplane mode, open the Q-Tech icon.
- The app still opens (it may say **You are offline** when you try to save). Turn Airplane mode off. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

## Part B: iPhone, Safari (about 7 minutes)

iPhone cannot share into Q-Tech from other apps, so you copy in the other app and paste into Capture. This is expected.

**B1. Open the app.** Open **Safari** (not Chrome). Go to `qtechcrm.vercel.app` and sign in.
- You should see the home screen with the green **Capture** button. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**B2. Add to Home Screen.** Tap the **Share** square-with-arrow button at the bottom of Safari, scroll down, tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**. (A message "Add Q-Tech CRM to your home screen: Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen" may appear first; that is the same thing.)
- A **Q-Tech** icon appears on the home screen. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**B3. Open from the icon.** Tap the **Q-Tech** icon.
- Opens full screen, no Safari bar. If it asks you to sign in again, do so (that is OK on iPhone). [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**B4. Copy a message.** Go to WhatsApp, press and hold a customer message, tap **Copy**. Go back to the Q-Tech app. Tap the green **Capture** button.
- The Capture sheet opens with the title **Capture** and a box saying **Tap and hold here, then tap Paste**. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**B5. Paste.** Tap and hold in that box, tap **Paste** (if iPhone asks "Allow Paste", tap **Allow Paste**).
- Your message text appears. The line under "What should we do with it?" lets you choose. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**B6. Save to an RFQ.** Tap **Add to RFQ notes**, tap your test RFQ, tap **Add to notes**. Count taps from tapping Capture to "Saved to": ______ taps.
- You see **Saved to** and the RFQ number. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**B7. Pick a photo.** Tap **Capture** again, then tap **Choose photos**, pick a photo from your library.
- The photo appears under **What you captured**. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.

**B8. Attach it to an order.** Tap **Attach to an order**, tap your test order, tap **Attach to order**.
- You see **Saved to** and the order number. [ ]
- If it doesn't work, screenshot and send.
