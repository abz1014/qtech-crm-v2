Method:
- The branches were extracted with `git archive` into `scratchpad/audit2/src-*`.
- I read the shell, every `src/ui` primitive and the e2e and gallery axe specs.
- Feature screens were reviewed by targeted reading and by pattern sweeps (raw inputs, `opacity-0` overlays, `aria-label` on generic elements, fixed widths, sticky and fixed bars, `truncate`, colour classes, live regions).
- I did no runtime testing. Items marked **verify** need a browser.

Reference viewports used below:
- **"200% on 1366"**: about 683 × 330 CSS px. That is below `md`, so the phone layout with `pointer: fine`.
- **150% on 1366**: about 911 px wide.
- **175% on 1366**: about 780 px wide.

## 1. Headline

- **Overall: 7/10 on main. It partially conforms to WCAG 2.2 AA.** The base is better than usual:
  - Radix dialogs and popovers trap focus and return it.
  - Every `Field` wires `label`, `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`.
  - Status is always words plus glyph plus colour, from the `src/lib/status.ts` registry.
  - There is a skip link, real landmarks, one `h1` per page, `lang="en"`, motion tokens that drop to 0.01 ms under reduced motion, and 44 px targets on touch.
- **The brief was partly wrong: axe already runs on feature screens.**
  - On main there are 113 `axeViolations` calls in 25 DB e2e specs. They cover Companies, RFQ board, intake, detail, quotation, memory and ranking, Today, search, orders, PO, split and repeat, expenses including the burst sheet, bank, month-end, costs, GST, receivables and reminders, reports, settings targets and categories, capture and the password page.
  - They mostly run at 1440 and 375, are WCAG 2.2 AA plus best-practice, and the bar is zero violations.
  - Lighthouse accessibility must also be at least 95 on 7 screens (`e2e/perf/perf.spec.ts`).
  - The gaps are what axe cannot see: keyboard behaviour, focus loss, focus hidden under sticky bars, zoom and reflow bands, live-region behaviour, and `aria-label` on role-less elements. axe reports those as *incomplete*, and the helper ignores incomplete results.
- **Real failures, most important first:**
  1. Global single-key shortcuts that cannot be turned off (N, C, G T, `/`). SC 2.1.4, level A.
  2. Busy buttons and fieldsets that drop keyboard focus. SC 2.4.3.
  3. No focus move, scroll reset or per-page title on route change. SC 2.4.3 and 2.4.2.
  4. Wide dialogs clip off-screen between 768 and about 1000 px, which is a 1366 laptop at 150–175% zoom. SC 1.4.10 and 1.4.4.
  5. DataTable hides columns at 640–1279 px with no other way to see the data. SC 1.4.10.
  6. Toasts are not reliably announced, and the Undo countdown re-announces every second. SC 4.1.3.
  7. Sticky and fixed action bars can cover the focused control. SC 2.4.11.
  8. Two places where native controls are `opacity-0` overlays, so focus is invisible. SC 2.4.7.
  9. About 35 `aria-label`s on role-less `div`, `span` and `p` elements are ignored by screen readers. SC 1.3.1.
- **The `ux/*` branches are net positive.**
  - ux/foundation is a large readability gain: 14–16 px text, darker greys, 36–44 px controls, and a palette that fits short screens. It has one regression: the selected segment of a SegmentedControl is now harder to tell from the others (SC 1.4.11).
  - ux/orders and ux/money-today carry over the generic `aria-label` pattern.
  - ux/orders adds a `position: fixed` bottom bar on phones with no scroll padding (SC 2.4.11 risk).
  - InfoTips now hold a couple of consequences that were printed on screen before.

## 7. Should axe be extended to feature screens in e2e? Yes, but differently than the brief assumed

axe already runs on almost every feature screen. The next gains come from five things.

1. **Make the shared helper fail on the high-value "incomplete" results** (`e2e/support/axe.ts:44-64`).
   - It requests `resultTypes: ['violations']`, so `aria-prohibited-attr` incompletes are dropped. Those incompletes are the about 35 ignored labels in fix 9.
   - Add `incomplete` and fail when `id === 'aria-prohibited-attr'`.
   - Log `color-contrast` incompletes (text over images or gradients) as warnings.
   - This takes one helper change and covers all 113 existing calls.
2. **Cover the screens and states that have no axe run.** Use `axeViolations(page, { landmarks: false })` for overlays.
   - Settings home, Team (add person, change role, deactivate, reset password, the phone RowActionSheet), My details (Text size panel), Company profile, Exchange rates.
   - Recurring expenses panel and dialog, Invoice detail dialog.
   - Sign-in with the error banners, No access, Not found.
   - Account menu open, one row menu open per list.
   - Today after "Copy and mark chased", with the Undo toast visible.
   - With `data-text="small"` once (foundation).
3. **Add a `zoom` viewport pass, which is the owner's real setup.**
   - Use these viewports: **683×330** (1366 at 200%), **780×600** (1366 at 175%), **1093×560** (1366 at 125%), and **320×256** (the WCAG reflow box) for the shell only.
   - For each main route (reuse the `screens()` list in `e2e/perf/perf.spec.ts`), assert:
     - `documentElement.scrollWidth <= innerWidth` and `#main.scrollWidth <= #main.clientWidth`. This extends `shell-overflow.spec.ts`.
     - Every open `[role=dialog]` rectangle lies inside the viewport. This catches fix 4.
     - No `th` that belongs to a visible table is `display:none` while its data is absent from the row, or simpler, snapshot the visible header names per width. This catches fix 5.
     - axe passes.
4. **Add a keyboard sweep helper, `focusSweep(page, n = 40)`.** It presses Tab n times. After each press it asserts that `document.activeElement`:
   - is not `body`;
   - has a non-zero rectangle inside `#main` or the open dialog;
   - shows a focus indicator (`outline-style !== 'none'`, or a `box-shadow`, or a `:has(:focus-visible)` ancestor outline). This catches fix 8.
   - is the top element at its centre (`document.elementFromPoint`), so it is not under a sticky or fixed bar. This catches fix 7, 2.4.11.

   It also presses a known busy action once and asserts that focus is not `body` afterwards (fix 2). Click a nav link and assert that focus is on the `h1` and `#main.scrollTop === 0` (fix 3, once built).
5. **Where to put it.**
   - Add one DB suite, `e2e/a11y/*.spec.ts`, as a new `a11y` project in `playwright.config.ts`. Set `baseURL` to `127.0.0.1:4173`, `fullyParallel: false`, signed in as admin and sales through `openAs`.
   - Add `a11y:` with `- 'e2e/a11y/**'` to `.github/e2e-suites.yml` (weight about 120). It then runs on every `src/**` change through `shared`.
   - Keep the per-feature axe calls where they are. The new suite owns the cross-cutting checks (zoom widths, keyboard sweep, overlays not yet covered) so feature specs do not each grow.
   - Separately, add a **unit design guard** in `src/lib/designGuards.test.ts` that computes the contrast of the selected and unselected segment tokens and of the focus ring on every tone background. axe cannot check state contrast (fix 11).

Not worth adding: axe against the PDFs (not applicable), or running the gallery axe at 683 px (the gallery is about primitives, and the zoom pass belongs to real screens).
